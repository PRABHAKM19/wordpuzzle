A QUICK FIELD GUIDE

Follow the letters.

  1. Find every word in the list before the timer runs out. Press Start puzzle or select a letter to begin.
  2. Drag from the first to the last letter, or click or tap the two endpoints. Every word follows a straight line.
  3. On a keyboard, use arrow keys to move, Enter or Space to select endpoints, and Escape to cancel a selection.
  4. Easy: 8 words, 6 minutes, across or down. Medium: 10 words, 5 minutes, all directions. Hard: 12 words, 4 minutes, all directions.
  5. Each hint highlights an unfound word’s first letter and costs 15 seconds. You get three per puzzle.

Use Pause for a break. The grid is hidden while paused. Switching tabs does not stop the clock.
